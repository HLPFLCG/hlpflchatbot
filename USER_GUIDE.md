# HLPFL Chatbot - User Guide

## Welcome to HLPFL Records Chatbot! 🎵

The HLPFL chatbot is your AI-powered assistant for learning about our artist-first music platform. Get instant answers about our services, company information, artist submissions, and music industry insights.

## Getting Started

### Access the Chatbot
- **Live Demo:** Visit the HLPFL website and click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner
- **24/7 Availability:** The chatbot is always ready to help you with information about HLPFL Records

### How to Use the Chatbot

1. **Open the Chat Widget**
   - Click the chat bubble icon in the bottom-right corner of the page
   - The chat window will open with a welcome message

2. **Ask Your Questions**
   - Type your question in the text input field
   - Press Enter or click the "Send" button
   - The chatbot will provide relevant information

3. **Use Quick Actions**
   - After receiving a response, you'll see quick action buttons
   - Click these to explore related topics
   - Examples: "Tell me about your services", "How do I submit music?"
